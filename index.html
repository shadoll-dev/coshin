    How to Use coShin

    coShin is a full-screen colour flasher designed for ambient lighting, mood setting, photography background effects, focus timing, or visual flashing sequences.

    Features & Options

    • Start / Stop: Press START to launch full-screen colour cycling. Press STOP at any time to return.
    • Timer Interval: Customise the display time per colour (from 0.5 to 60 seconds) in Settings (⚙).
    • Colour Order: Toggle between Random shuffling and Circle (in order) sequential cycling.
    • Custom Palette: Add any hex colour using the colour picker, or delete chips to tailor your palette.
    • Screen Sleep Prevention (Wake Lock): Automatically keeps your display active while flashing so your screen won't sleep unattended.
    • Colour History: Tracks the 10 most recent flashed colours in a top bar during active playback.
