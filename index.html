      How to Use coShin

      coShin is a full-screen colour flasher & stopwatch.

      Features & Options

      • Modes: Switch between Timer (auto-advancing countdown) and Stopwatch (count-up with lap logging) in Settings (⚙).
      • Stopwatch & Laps: Tap LAP (or anywhere on screen / Spacebar) to record lap split times and cycle to the next colour. Press STOP to pause and access RESUME, RESTART, or END.
      • Timer Interval: Customise the display time per colour (from 0.5 to 60 seconds) for Timer mode.
      • Colour Order: Toggle between Random shuffling and Circle (sequential order) cycling.
      • Custom Palette: Add any custom hex colour using the colour picker, or remove chips to tailor your palette.
      • Screen Sleep Prevention (Wake Lock): Keeps your display active while flashing so your screen won't sleep unattended.
      • Colour History: Tracks recent flashed colours in a top bar during Timer mode playback.
